pub fn allocate_budget(
    groups: &[BudgetGroup<'_>],
    budget: usize,
    criterion: BudgetCriterion,
) -> Result<BudgetAllocation, RouteError>
Expand description

Share budget facilities among groups, given what each further facility adds in each group.

With BudgetCriterion::Utilitarian each facility goes to the group where the next one adds most. As the gains of a group do not increase, this covers the most demand possible with every budget up to budget (marginal analysis: Fox, 1966). With BudgetCriterion::Maximin each facility goes to the group with the lowest share of its demand covered that still has a facility to open, which gives the highest possible lowest share with every budget. Ties go to the larger gain, then to the group listed first. Fewer than budget facilities come back when no group has a facility left that adds anything.

Comparing the covered of the two criteria for the same budget gives what the fairer allocation costs (Bertsimas, Farias and Trichakis, 2011).

Errors (RouteError::Invalid): a gain that is negative, not finite or larger than the one before it in its group; a covered that is negative or not finite; a total that is negative or infinite; and BudgetCriterion::Maximin with a total that is NaN.
