The two-step floating catchment area of Luo and Wang (2003) and, with a decay function, its enhanced form (Luo and Qi, 2009).

Step 1: each facility’s supply is divided by the demand within its catchment, each demand point weighted by the decay of its travel time. Step 2: each demand point adds up the ratios of the facilities within its catchment, weighted the same way. costs gives the minutes from each demand point (row) to each facility (column), demand the weight of each demand point and supply the capacity of each facility.

Errors: RouteError::Invalid for weights or capacities that do not match the table, are negative or are not finite, a cutoff that is not set, or a decay parameter out of range; RouteError::Cancelled.
