Expand description

peabiru’s multi-criteria trip planner: for each origin-destination pair (origins[i] to destinations[i], (lat, lon) degrees), the trips not dominated on the chosen criteria at each departure minute of the window, merged over the window into options.

At each minute the result is the exact Pareto set of the trips the network allows (the direct trip by the street mode, and public transport trips from the stops of the access search to those of the egress search, boarding at least 60 s after reaching a stop), one trip per vector of values: a trip is dominated when another is no worse on every chosen criterion and better on one; of trips with the same values on the chosen criteria, the one with the least total time, then transfers, time on foot and fare is kept. Of that exact set, the trips are then compared with total time to a tenth of a minute and walking to TripOptionsParams::walk_resolution (a minute by default), and those beaten in those terms are left out; the values returned are exact. There are no random draws: frequency-based trips are boarded at their expected times (half a headway of waiting; the scheduled departures with exact_times = 1), so the result does not change from call to call and differs on purpose from the Monte Carlo draws of the other functions. See the guide of the packages for the definitions and how this differs from pareto_frontier() and detailed_itineraries().

Errors: invalid parameters, the fare criterion without a fare structure, cancellation, and a car search that cannot be routed reproducibly (see detailed_itineraries).
