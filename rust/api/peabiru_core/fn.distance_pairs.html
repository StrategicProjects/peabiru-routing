Expand description

Every pair of a point of from and a point of to at most max_distance metres apart in a straight line, with the distance.

The points are (lon, lat) in degrees and the distance is the great-circle one on a sphere of radius 6,367,450 m, the same that measures the streets of a network. A pair at distance 0 is a pair. The result goes into CostTable::from_pairs as it is, the metres as the cost.

Errors: RouteError::Invalid for a coordinate that is not finite, a latitude outside -90 to 90, a max_distance that is negative or not finite, or more than u32::MAX points in a set; RouteError::Cancelled.
