Travel-time surface by a street mode alone (WALK, BICYCLE or CAR), the first step of a polygon isochrone: one travel time per cell, the same at every percentile. The grid is WebMercatorGrid::for_network at zoom (9 to 12). params.max_trip_duration is the search limit (for isochrones, the largest cutoff plus 10 minutes). Points are (lat, lon) degrees; an origin that cannot be linked reaches nothing. RouteError::Invalid for invalid parameters, percentiles (see validate_percentiles) or zoom, RouteError::Cancelled when params.cancel is cancelled.

Most of a first call from a few origins is linking the grid’s cells to the streets. The network keeps those links for later surfaces on the same grid and mode (public transport surfaces walk, so they share the walking ones): up to 9 grids and modes, the least recently used dropped first beyond 64 MiB, at 40 bytes a cell (6.2 MiB for the Porto Alegre sample at zoom 12, 0.4 MiB at zoom 10). Clones of the network share them.
