Expand description

The trip limits of accessibility(), derived from what the user asked. Given the user’s max_trip_duration and max_walk_time (None = Inf) in minutes, returns the (max_trip_duration, max_walk_time) to route with:

  • max_trip_duration may not be below the largest cutoff (an error);
  • with the step function it becomes the largest cutoff;
  • max_walk_time = Inf then becomes that max_trip_duration;
  • for walking alone (walk_only, no transit) max_trip_duration is reduced to max_walk_time. (Bicycle and car alone have their own limits, which also default to max_trip_duration.)
