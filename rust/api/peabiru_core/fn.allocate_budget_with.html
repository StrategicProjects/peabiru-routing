Expand description

allocate_budget with weights, priority tiers and groups left out (BudgetRules).

With weights, BudgetCriterion::Utilitarian gives each facility to the group where the next one adds most once multiplied by the weight of the group: the weighted gains of a group still do not increase, so this is the largest weighted sum for every budget. BudgetCriterion::Maximin gives it to the group with the lowest share covered divided by its weight. With tiers the criterion only compares the groups of the lowest tier that still has a facility to open that adds something. A group that is not eligible gets none.

The gains, the demand covered and the lowest share of the result are never weighted and take in every group, eligible or not, so that runs with and without the rules can be compared.

Errors (RouteError::Invalid): those of allocate_budget; a list of BudgetRules whose length is not the number of groups; and a weight that is not finite or not above zero.
